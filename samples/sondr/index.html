A few good answers.

How do I connect?+

The speakers, Air Buds and Arc Headphones connect over Bluetooth. Form One and Arc also have a 3.5 mm wired input. Rise Charger uses USB-C power and a compatible magnetic wireless-charging phone.

Which accessories fit my speaker?+

The desk stand and travel case are designed for Form One. They are available in its configuration panel. Other SONDR devices do not use these accessories. Rise Charger is for compatible phones and does not charge SONDR audio devices.

What comes in the box?+

Speakers include a USB-C cable and quick-start guide. Air Buds include a charging case and three ear-tip sizes. Arc Headphones include a pouch and audio cable. Rise includes a USB-C cable; its required 20 W or higher wall adapter is sold separately.

How do I care for the finish?+

Use a soft, dry cloth. Keep liquids away from the grille and charging port, and avoid abrasive cleaners. These devices are not intended for wet conditions.
