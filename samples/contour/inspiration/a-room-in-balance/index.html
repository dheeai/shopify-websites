A room in balance.

Build around one piece you love. Give everything else a reason to be there.

IN THIS GUIDE

01 / Start with the anchor.02 / Leave room between forms.03 / Use a rug to connect the seating.Measure your space
01

Start with the anchor.

Choose the largest piece first—a sofa, dining table or cabinet. Its width, depth and position establish the scale of the room. Measure it before choosing the pieces around it.

02

Leave room between forms.

A low table can balance a generous sofa, while an open space beside a chair keeps the arrangement from feeling crowded. Mark furniture footprints on the floor and walk through the room before settling on a layout.

03

Use a rug to connect the seating.

Try a rug that reaches beneath the front feet of your seating. Compare its outline with the actual furniture dimensions; the right size depends on the arrangement, not just the room’s overall area.
