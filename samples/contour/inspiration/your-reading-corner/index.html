SMALL SPACES / 03

A corner worth keeping.

A chair, a surface and a light. Start small and make the space your own.

IN THIS GUIDE

01 / Find the natural pause.02 / Put the essentials within reach.03 / Give the light a purpose.Measure your space
01

Find the natural pause.

Look for a place away from the main route through the room. A corner near a window can work well, provided curtains and doors still open freely.

02

Put the essentials within reach.

Place a small table beside the chair for a book or a cup. Check the table height against the seat and arm, and try the distance while seated rather than judging it from across the room.

03

Give the light a purpose.

Position a reading light where it illuminates the page without shining into your eyes. Check cable access and the fixture’s specifications, then add just one personal detail—a vase, a favourite book or a cushion.
