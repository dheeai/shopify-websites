MATERIALS / 02

Warmth, without the clutter.

A small palette can carry a whole room. Let grain, texture and shape do the work.

IN THIS GUIDE

01 / Choose a common thread.02 / Balance smooth and textured.03 / Repeat, then leave a pause.Measure your space
01

Choose a common thread.

Start with a wood tone you enjoy. Add another timber with a similar warmth, or make the contrast deliberate. Natural oak and deeper walnut can sit together when the surrounding colours stay simple.

02

Balance smooth and textured.

Pair a broad timber surface with a textured fabric or a small stone object. Different surfaces add interest without requiring more colours or more furniture.

03

Repeat, then leave a pause.

Echo one material in two places—a table and a frame, for example. Leave some surfaces clear so those connections are easy to see. A room needs space around its objects as much as it needs objects.
