THE MATERIAL LIBRARY

Character you
can feel.

A closer look at the surfaces, textures, and tones that shape a room.

01 / Oak

Light, with character.

Open grain and a lighter tone bring an easy warmth to a room. Pair oak with linen, stone or a darker accent.

Caring for oak

Dust with a soft cloth, protect from heat and wipe spills promptly.

Explore oak pieces

02 / Walnut

A deeper kind of warmth.

Rich colour and expressive grain give simple forms their depth. A little walnut can anchor a lighter space.

Caring for walnut

Use coasters and avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

Explore walnut pieces

03 / Textile

The softer side.

Bouclé and woven fabrics add depth to a room. Layer similar tones or introduce a contrasting colour.

Caring for textile

Vacuum gently and follow the care notes for each individual piece.

Explore textile pieces

04 / Stone

Naturally individual.

Travertine brings a quiet, tactile presence. Natural variation in tone and texture is part of its character.

Caring for stone

Avoid acidic cleaners; use a soft cloth and pH-neutral stone care.

Explore stone pieces

05 / Ceramic

Shape, glaze, presence.

Sculptural forms and rich glazes give lamps and small objects their character.

Caring for ceramic

Handle carefully and clean by hand.

Explore ceramic pieces

06 / Metal

A line of light.

Brass tones bring warmth to slim stems and curved frames. Pair them with opal shades or natural wood.

Caring for metal

Disconnect lighting before cleaning. Dust with a dry cloth and avoid abrasive polish on coated finishes.

Explore metal pieces

07 / Glass

Light, softly diffused.

Opal glass softens the glow of a pendant or floor lamp. Its rounded shape balances more angular furniture.

Caring for glass

Switch off and allow the fixture to cool before cleaning. Dust with a soft, dry cloth.

Explore glass pieces
