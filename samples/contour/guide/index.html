A LITTLE PLANNING

Make room
for the right fit.

Check the room, the furniture and the route from your front door.

01

Start with your space.

Measure the available floor area, noting radiators, doors and fixed features. Check how doors open and where you walk.

02

Mark out the footprint.

Use the product’s width and depth to mark its outline on the floor. Try the arrangement before committing to a larger piece.

03

Consider the journey.

Measure doorways, hallways, stairs and lifts. Packaging and assembly requirements also affect access; a floor plan alone cannot confirm delivery fit.
